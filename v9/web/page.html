Flight model

Switch models to compare: the autopilot is the same; the 6DOF model adds real dynamics (phugoid, Dutch roll, adverse yaw, propeller torque, gear loads) that the control laws must handle. Both run in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly.

Autopilot

Pitch-for-speed climb, pitch-for-path approach with throttle on speed. Below 300 ft a stabilised-approach gate triggers a go-around after 2 s outside limits. In a crosswind the approach is flown crabbed, then below 60 ft rudder aligns the nose and the into-wind wing goes down. Every command is rate-limited, so no mode change steps a control.

Short-field technique

Brakes held until full power, rotate at 48 kt with 10° pitch, best-angle climb at 57 kt to 100 ft. Steep 5.5° power-on approach at 60 kt aimed 40 m past the threshold, firm touchdown without float, flaps up and full braking. The 600 m and 450 m strips have 50 ft trees 150 m beyond each end; the landing check scores distances and tree clearance.

Engine failure

On the runway it rejects; below 700 ft after take-off it lands ahead; higher up it plans a power-off return to runway 09 or 27, re-checking every second, and lands ahead if neither is reachable. The High circuit keeps the runway within glide reach for longer.
