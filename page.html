Mode log

The autopilot nests three layers: attitude hold (pitch and bank), flight-path, speed and track loops, and a mode manager that steps through the circuit. Climb flies pitch-for-speed at full power; the approach flies pitch-for-glidepath with throttle on speed. On final below 300 ft it monitors a stabilised-approach gate (speed −5/+10 kt, glidepath ±0.7°, centreline ±30 m, sink under 1,000 fpm, bank under 15°) and goes around if any is breached for 2 seconds, or if the flare floats past the first third of the runway. In a crosswind it flies the approach crabbed, then below 60 ft it straightens up: rudder swings the nose onto the runway heading while the into-wind wing goes down to stop the drift, so HDG and TRK match at touchdown. Every command is rate-limited (pitch 3°/s, bank 8°/s, throttle 30%/s, flaps 3°/s), so mode changes, including a go-around, ramp rather than step. The High circuit (default) holds 1,000 ft on a tighter downwind until the 5.5° approach path meets it, so the runway stays within power-off gliding reach for much more of the flight; the GLIDE REACH readout and the strip under the altitude trace show when a return would make it, using either runway direction. Press Fail engine at any point: on the runway it rejects the take-off; below 700 ft after take-off it lands straight ahead with no turn-back; higher up it plans a power-off return to runway 09 or 27, whichever leaves more height in hand (base key point, intercept, aim 300 m in) from height, speed, wind and glide ratio, re-checking every second, and lands ahead if the runway is out of reach. On a power-off final it adds flap in stages, forward-slips if still high, and straightens up for touchdown. Raise the crosswind, turn on gusts or press Go around to stress it.
