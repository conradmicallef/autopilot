Flight model

The autopilot's control laws fly it through the real dynamics: phugoid, Dutch roll, adverse yaw, propeller torque, gear loads. It runs in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly.

Autopilot

Pitch-for-speed climb, pitch-for-path approach with throttle on speed. Below 300 ft a stabilised-approach gate triggers a go-around after 2 s outside limits. In a crosswind the approach is flown crabbed, then below 60 ft rudder aligns the nose and the into-wind wing goes down. Turbulence is measured from the wind estimate (GPS minus air data): it sets the gust margin on the approach speed, softens the short-field flare, and adds power in the flare if a lull drops the speed below 1.3 Vs. Every command is rate-limited, so no mode change steps a control.

Short-field technique

Brakes held until full power, rotate at 48 kt with 10° pitch, best-angle climb at 57 kt to 100 ft. Steep 5.5° power-on approach at 60 kt aimed 40 m past the threshold, firm touchdown without float, flaps up and full braking. The 600 m and 450 m strips have 50 ft trees 150 m beyond each end; the landing check scores distances and tree clearance.

Engine failure

On the runway it rejects; below 700 ft after take-off it lands ahead; higher up it plans a power-off return to runway 09 or 27, re-checking every second, and lands ahead if neither is reachable. Lined up on final it always goes for the runway, raising the flap if short. Failing the engine drops the speed to 1×. The High circuit keeps the runway within glide reach for longer.
